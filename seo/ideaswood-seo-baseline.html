What blocks page 1

In order of impact.

  1. Google can't tell which site is the company. ideaswood.eu and ideaswood.com carry the same pages with no hreflang, so they compete with each other. The old www shop, factory.ideaswood.eu ("logcabinsfactory.com") and business.ideaswood.eu all compete. There are also 2 Facebook pages, 2 Instagram accounts, 2 phone numbers, 2 addresses and 2 founding years (1993 and 2007). The registered company (IDEAS for PEOPLE, UAB, about 2 employees) doesn't match the "factory" claims.
  2. The site structure is broken. Log cabins sit in 3 separate category trees, 10 categories look like empty or error pages to Google, and 23 old URLs were never redirected.
  3. Page titles can't rank. Products are named only by model ("Verno", "Kaia") without saying what they are, and categories use "Buy … in UK" while prices are in EUR.
  4. Key pages are missing. Competitors have pages for "log cabin manufacturer", wholesale, wall thickness (28/44/70mm), UK and Ireland delivery, costs and planning permission. Ideaswood has none of these.
  5. No trust signals from other sites. No reviews, no directory listings, no backlinks. For "log cabin manufacturer Lithuania", Google ranks ideaswood's Facebook page #1, but not the website.

## Permitted development rules for outbuildings in England
Check each one for your garden:
1. **Position.** The cabin must not stand in front of the main wall of the house that faces a road.
2. **Height.** Single storey. Eaves no higher than 2.5 m. Total height no more than 4 m with a dual-pitched roof, or 3 m with any other roof.
3. **Near a boundary.** If any part is within 2 m of a boundary, the total height must be no more than 2.5 m.
4. **Size of the garden used.** Outbuildings and other additions must not cover more than half the land around the original house.
5. **Platforms.** No raised platform higher than 30 cm. No verandas or balconies.
6. **Use.** The cabin must be for use that goes with the house, such as an office, gym, studio, playroom or storage. Not a separate home, and not somewhere you sleep as a normal arrangement.
7. **Designated land.** In a National Park, National Landscape (AONB), conservation area or World Heritage Site, an outbuilding more than 20 m from the house is limited to 10 m² of floor area. Other limits can also be stricter.
8. **Listed buildings.** No permitted development for outbuildings in the grounds of a listed building. You need permission.

When any rule does not fit, apply to your local planning authority, or ask them for a lawful development certificate to confirm that your plan is allowed.

Google currently sees the same English pages on .eu and .com (same slugs, same guide), so it picks one and filters the other, and brand search splits across 4 domains. Without signals, .eu and .com compete with each other for every keyword.
