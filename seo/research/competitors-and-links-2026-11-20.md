# Competitor teardown and link/citation plan (2026-11-20)

Goal: get ideaswood.eu onto Google page 1 for product and supplier searches (log cabins, garden rooms, saunas, camping pods; UK, Ireland, EU; plus B2B resellers). Starting point: no reviews, no backlinks, average position 68.

## Method and limits (read first)
- WebFetch could not reach any competitor site in this session (DNS failure for summerhouse24.co.uk, gardenbuildingsdirect.co.uk, tigersheds.com, palmako.co.uk, loghouse.ie, tuin.co.uk). Those hosts need to be allowed in the session network settings if a page-level read is wanted.
- Everything below comes from WebSearch (AI-written summary plus result titles and URLs). Therefore:
  - Page titles are VERIFIED only where quoted from the result list. Exact H1 text, intro word counts, filter sets and Trustpilot scores were NOT seen and are marked "not verified".
  - Prices, discounts and delivery claims are competitor self-claims from search snapshots (Black Friday period), so treat them as indicative.
- "summerhouse24 etc." in the brief: summerhouse24 is covered once (it was listed twice). No other competitors were added.
- Wording rules applied: IdeasWood is a supplier and exporter working with contracted factories (not a manufacturer). No FSC/PEFC or CE claims anywhere. No fake reviews, no paid-link schemes.
- Verified = the URL appeared in a search result this session. It does NOT mean the page was read, or that the offer suits IdeasWood.

### Cross-competitor patterns
- Size and use landing pages ("8x6 log cabins", "under 2.5 m", "20x12") are normal and rank.
- Everyone leads with a guarantee length and "free delivery".
- Only tuin.co.uk (2,200+ Trustpilot reviews) and loghouse.ie (showrooms, county pages) have strong third-party/local trust. IdeasWood has neither yet.
- Saunas: palmako and tuin have real sauna category pages; tigersheds treats saunas as an add-on, so a sauna category with kit specs is the least crowded entry.

### Ten things IdeasWood category pages should copy or beat
1. Title formula: "[Product] for Sale UK & Ireland | [differentiator] | IdeasWood" (e.g. "Log Cabins for Sale UK & Ireland - Supplier Direct"). Do not claim "manufacturer". Include "supplier and exporter" in the intro, not the title.
2. Size landing pages: build /log-cabins/4x3m, /2-5x2m, /5x4m etc. as indexable pages, with metric first and imperial in the title ("4m x 3m / 13x10ft"). Gardenbuildingsdirect and Tiger rank with this. Needs unique intro per size (min 80 words) to avoid thin pages.
3. Specification in titles and cards: wall thickness (mm), m2, glazing, roof type, bedrooms/bathroom where true (summerhouse24, palmako pattern). Product cards should show these as text, not images only.
4. Guarantee and warranty line above the fold, but only what the contracted factories really back in writing. Show the exact period and what it covers; link to the terms page. Avoid certification claims (no CE, FSC, PEFC).
5. Delivery clarity: stated lead time ranges per product, delivery zones UK/IE/EU (Ireland is a gap: loghouse.ie publishes EUR 1.70/km), and "delivered as pre-cut kit, self-assembly" wording if that is true. Add an optional installation partner list if one exists.
6. Finance/price transparency: competitors show Novuna finance (up to 48 months) and VAT-inclusive prices. Only add finance if a real provider is signed; otherwise beat them on a clear "what is and is not included" box (foundations, assembly, electrics, delivery), echoing the cost-guide page already on ideaswood.eu.
7. Reviews: start collecting genuine verified reviews (see Part 2: Trustpilot, Google). Until there are at least 10 real ones, do not show star ratings or review schema; show "What customers ask" and factory/project photos instead.
8. Category intro of about 150-250 words above the grid (what the category is, who it is for, materials, typical price range, who IdeasWood is), plus a longer buying guide (500+ words) below the grid with internal links. Competitor intro lengths were not verified, so test and measure.
9. Sauna and camping-pod category pages with kit-level detail: barrel vs cabin vs pod, wall thickness, heater compatibility, capacity, shipping to Ireland. This is the least contested area (tigersheds has none, tuin/palmako lightly).
10. Guides linked from each category: planning rules UK and Ireland (Irish rules are in flux: current 25 m2 exemption with reports of proposed increases, so link to official sources and state "check current rules"), cost guide, insulation/wall-thickness guide, foundations guide, sauna buying guide. Each guide must link to a category and be linked from it.

Also worth copying: county/region landing pages (loghouse.ie) only where IdeasWood truly serves and can deliver; do not create doorway pages.

### Things we should not do
- No fake, staff-written, gifted-for-review or "review gating" practices. Trustpilot guidelines (https://legal.trustpilot.com/for-reviewers/guidelines-for-businesses, verified) forbid selective invitations and incentives.
- No buying links, link swaps, comment spam, PBNs or bulk low-quality directory submissions (Google spam policies above). Paid placements must be marked rel="sponsored" or nofollow.
- Do not describe IdeasWood as a manufacturer, and do not mention FSC/PEFC or CE on any profile. Describe goods as "supplied via contracted factories".
- Do not copy competitor claims such as inconsistent customer counts or guarantee periods; only publish what IdeasWood can document.
