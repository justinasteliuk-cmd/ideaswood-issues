## Causes and fixes (for the Mac executor / Saqib)
1. ideaswood.eu category and product pages, CLS 1.0 to 1.9: the Divi Theme Builder body section (et_pb_section_0_tb_body / et_builder_inner_content product) shifts when it renders late. Give the product gallery/summary and the category grid fixed height or aspect-ratio placeholders, make sure the late-loading gallery (Lepton) and the add-ons form do not push content down.
2. Product page LCP element is the image eco-vs-pre-png.webp (805x824, no alt): give it width/height, fetchpriority/preload or move it below the fold, add alt text. Also check what it shows (flagged earlier).
3. accounts.google.com/gsi/client (Google Sign-In, 101 KiB) loads on every page: remove or load only on login pages.
4. Divi admin fonts modules.woff (90 KiB) and fontawesome 78 KiB on the front end: disable Divi icon font loading or limit to used icons.
5. Render-blocking resources cost about 1.4-1.5 s on category/product pages: enable Divi critical CSS/static CSS, LiteSpeed CSS combine + async, defer unused JS (about 230 KiB unused).
6. Images: use WebP everywhere (about 360 KiB savings on category), real responsive sizes, lazy-load below the fold (1.1 MB offscreen on product page). Category listing uses 300x300 PNGs of 130-160 KiB: convert.
7. timberescapes.com.au: 13.7 MB home, PNG/JPG up to 3.7 MB each (E2222-GS-Hintergrund-fur-Glasfront-3.png 3.7 MB, Typ-2-52x102-5.jpg 2.2 MB). Convert to WebP and resize to display width: about 3.9 MB savings plus 1 MB responsive. Target under 2 MB.
8. abitazionidilegno.it: home 8.9 MB. The biggest image is hotlinked from ideaswood.com (a-frame.png 3.5 MB) plus 500-630 KB product images: compress and resize (about 3.9 MB responsive-image savings), host its own copies. CLS 0.215 on the hero column: set image dimensions.
9. ideaswood.com home is the best (74): same image rules.
Why it matters: Core Web Vitals are a ranking factor on mobile and slow category pages lose the sauna impressions we just started getting.
