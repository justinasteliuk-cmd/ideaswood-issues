## Contradictions or things to check

1. **No official page was read.** Every figure needs a manual check against the official URL before publication.
2. **Ireland 2026 change:** several secondary sources say the garden-structure limit rose from 25 m² to 30 m² from 27 July 2026; official text unseen, and S.I. numbering conflicts (338-344 / 339 / 340). Today is after that date, so the 30 m² figure may now be the current one. Confirm on irishstatutebook.ie, gov.ie or the SDCC page.
3. **Irish building regulations exemption for sheds:** nothing found. Do not state a figure.
4. **England vs Wales 4 m roof wording:** Wales page said "dual-pitched or hipped"; England says "dual pitched". Wales also has a 1.5 m limit within 2 m of the house, which I did not see for England.
5. **Conservation areas:** England's 20 m/10 m² rule applies to "article 2(3) land", which includes conservation areas, and a side-of-house ban applies there. Search summaries listed conservation areas inconsistently. Check Class E.1 and E.2 text.
6. **Incidental use and sleeping:** whether a garden room used as a bedroom is still "incidental" is a planning question separate from the building regulations sleeping-accommodation rule. Wording not verified.
7. **Scotland:** planning numbers not retrieved; building warrant exceptions for the 8-30 m² band unseen.
8. **Northern Ireland planning:** only a third-party page quoting a council document. Nidirect and the GPDO (NI) 2015 text not read.
9. **Wales building regulations amendment 2026** (WGC 003/2026) not read; may affect thresholds or fire provisions.
10. **England 2025-2026 changes:** none found, but the gov.uk technical guidance and Planning Portal pages could not be opened to confirm the current edition date. Also check for Article 4 directions and the Planning and Infrastructure Act 2025 secondary legislation.
11. **Part P:** Approved Document P URL is from memory; search returned only council notes.
