## H1: Log Cabin Delivery to the UK, Ireland and Europe
We deliver our log cabins, garden buildings and saunas as kits to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe. Transport is calculated individually for each order, because the price depends on what you order and where it goes.

## How to get a transport quote
Send us:
1. the model or models you want, and any options;
2. the full delivery address, with postcode;
3. how the vehicle can reach the site: road width, gate, slope, and whether you can unload there;
4. when you want delivery, if you have a date in mind.

We reply with the transport price for your order. Contact us through [link: contact page].

## What affects the transport price
- **Where it goes.** The distance and the country.
- **What you order.** The size and weight of the kit, and how many items.
- **Access to your site.** A narrow lane or a long carry from the road can change how the delivery is arranged.

## What the cabin price covers
Transport is not a fixed line on the product page. It is calculated for each order. Insulation and other options are priced separately from the cabin, so choose them first and then ask for the transport quote for the full order.

## UK and Ireland
- **Ireland** is in the EU, so there is no customs declaration.
- **The UK** is outside the EU. Deliveries to the UK need a customs declaration and import VAT is charged. We explain who handles each part in your quote.

## Before the delivery
1. **Prepare a level base.** A concrete slab, paving or ground screws. See [link: easy installation].
2. **Clear the access.** The vehicle needs to reach as close as possible. Tell us about any limits.
3. **Plan the unloading and storage.** Keep the packs dry and off the ground until assembly.
4. **Check the rules.** In England, many garden buildings do not need planning permission, but check height and boundary distance. See [link: planning permission guide].

## On the day
- Check the packages against the delivery note before the driver leaves.
- Photograph and note any visible damage on the delivery note, and tell us the same day.

## Delivery questions
**How much does delivery cost?** It is calculated for each order. Send us the details above for a price.
**Is transport included in the cabin price?** Transport is calculated individually for each order.
**Do you deliver to Ireland and the UK?** Yes. Ireland is in the EU. For the UK, customs and import VAT apply and we explain them in the quote.
**Can you deliver to a difficult site?** Tell us about the access when you ask for the quote.
**Is insulation included?** No. Insulation and other options are priced separately.
