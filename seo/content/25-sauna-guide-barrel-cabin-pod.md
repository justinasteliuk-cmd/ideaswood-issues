## H1: Barrel Sauna, Sauna Cabin or Sauna Pod: How to Choose

*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

**Short answer:** pick a barrel sauna for the classic round shape and a small footprint, or a long barrel with a changing room; a sauna pod for the most compact square-sided sauna in a corner or on a terrace; and a garden sauna cabin when you want more floor space, larger glazing, or two or three rooms for changing and cooling down.

Below we compare the three types with the real sizes of the models we supply, then go through the questions that usually decide it.

## What is the difference between a barrel, a cabin and a pod?

- **Barrel sauna.** A round timber tube held by tightening bands, lying on its side. Our barrels are 1.97 m across with 44 mm walls, stainless steel bands and a bitumen-shingle roof. The round shape keeps the room compact around the benches.
- **Sauna pod.** A small, square-sided sauna room. Ours is 2.4 x 1.9 m and 2.6 m high, built from pine panels with 44 mm walls and a 28 mm floor.
- **Sauna cabin.** A small timber building with upright walls and a roof, like a garden cabin with a sauna room inside. Cabins give the most choice in size, layout and glazing.

Not better, different. A barrel is compact and round, from 1.7 m long. A cabin gives more floor space, larger glazing and, on Sauna L and XL, two or three rooms.

### What is the difference between a sauna pod and a barrel sauna?
Our pod is square-sided, 2.4 x 1.9 m and 2.6 m high. Our barrels are round, 1.97 m across and 2.08 m high, in 1.7 m or 5.9 m lengths. Both small versions are listed for 2-4 people.
