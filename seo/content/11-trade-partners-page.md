## H1: Trade Partner Programme: Wholesale Log Cabins and Garden Buildings
IdeasWood has supplied log cabins, garden buildings and saunas since 2007. If you sell garden buildings in your own country, you can become a trade partner and buy at wholesale prices.

**[Button: Apply to become a partner → partner registration page]**

## How it works
1. **Apply.** Register as a partner. We check your company details.
2. **Sign the trade agreement.** After it is signed, you get access to the wholesale price list for our range.
3. **Order what you need.** You can order even a single product. There is no minimum order quantity.
4. **Agree the transport.** We calculate transport individually for each order, so you pay for the delivery you actually need.

## Who can apply
Retailers and resellers that operate in another country than Lithuania: garden building shops, garden centres, online shops, holiday-park suppliers and builders who resell. If you are a private buyer, see our [link: garden cabins] for retail prices.

## What you get
- Wholesale prices for partners, shown after the trade agreement is signed.
- Order from one product up: no minimum quantity.
- The full range: [link: garden cabins], [link: summer houses and chalets], [link: sheds and storage cabins], [link: saunas], [link: camping pods], [link: BBQ grill cabins], [link: A-frame cabins] and [link: garden furniture].
- Transport quoted for each order.
- Options such as insulation priced separately, so you can quote your own customers exactly what they choose.

## Prices and options
Wholesale prices are not shown on this page. They are shared with partners after the agreement. Insulation and other options are priced separately from the cabin. Transport is calculated for each order.

## Trade partner questions
**Is there a minimum order?** No. A partner can order even one product.
**How do I get the wholesale price list?** Apply, then sign the trade agreement. The price list is shared after that.
**Can I order only one cabin to start?** Yes.
**How is transport priced?** We calculate it individually for each order.
**Is insulation included in the price?** No. Insulation and other options are priced separately.
