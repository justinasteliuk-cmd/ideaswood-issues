Compact timber pods built from pine or thermowood, with a solid floor, double-glazed window and French-style double-glazed door. Three sizes, all 2.4 m wide. The roof is covered with bitumen shingles in black, red, green or grey.

### Specification (all three)
- Total height 2.6 m
- Side-wall boards 46 mm, floor boards 28 mm (4.0 m model: roof boards 46 mm)
- Timber: pine or thermowood
- 1 opening double-glazed window 700 x 900 mm in the back wall
- French-style double-glazed door 1030 x 1900 mm, finger-jointed pine, with lock and handle
- Roof: bitumen shingles

### Standard set and extras
Standard set: structure, solid floor, window, door. Furniture is not included: double bed, table and stool (3.0/3.5 m), or double bed, table and 3 benches, one extendable for sleeping (4.0 m), are available as extras. Insulation and other options are priced separately. Transport is calculated individually.

### For campsites and resellers
Wholesale prices go to B2B partners (resellers in other countries) after a trade agreement is signed, with no minimum order: even one pod. Ask for a quote with your country and number of pods.
