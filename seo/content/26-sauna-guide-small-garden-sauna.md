## H1: Small Garden Sauna: Sizes, Base and What to Prepare

*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

**Short answer:** a small garden sauna for 2-4 people needs a footprint of about 3.4 to 8 m². Our smallest is the 1.7 m barrel (1.97 x 1.7 m), then the 2.4 x 1.9 m sauna pod. Before delivery, prepare a firm, level, dry base, clear access, space around the sauna, and the electrics or a flue route for the heater.

This guide covers the sizes, how much space to leave, the base, the heater and the practical checks before your sauna arrives.

## Barrel, pod or small cabin: which suits a small garden?

- **The 1.7 m barrel** takes the least ground. Its round shape suits a narrow side return or the end of a patio.
- **The pod** is square-sided and 2.6 m high, so it has more headroom than the barrel and fits neatly against a fence line or into a corner.
- **Sauna S or Pulse** are small cabins: they need roughly twice the ground of the barrel but give upright walls and more room inside. Pulse includes a 0.64 m terrace.

If you have more space, the 5.9 m barrel and the larger cabins add a changing room. ## How much space do you need around the sauna?

The footprint is only the start. Mark out on the ground:

- **The outside size of the sauna**, plus room to walk all the way round it for assembly and later maintenance of the timber.
- **The door swing and a step out.** The pod and the 1.7 m barrel have no terrace, so plan paving or decking in front of the door.
- **Heater clearances.** With a wood stove, the flue needs the distances to walls, roof timber, trees and fences that the stove manual gives. Plan the sauna position around the flue.
- **Distance to boundaries and buildings.** Check local rules with your local authority before you order.
- **A cool-down spot.** A small bench or patio next to the sauna is where you will spend half the session.

## What base does a small garden sauna need?

Every sauna needs a **firm, level, dry base** under its full footprint. A base that is out of level makes doors bind and puts strain on the timber.

- **Sauna pod:** paving slabs or a small concrete slab are suitable bases [CHECK].
- **Barrel sauna:** a stable, level footing under the whole 1.7 m length.
- **Small cabins:** a level base under the full building size, such as 2.69 x 2.69 m for Sauna S.

Keep the base slightly above the surrounding ground so rainwater runs away and the timber does not sit in water. Ask us for the base details for the model you choose.

## Electric or wood-burning in a small garden?

In a small garden near the house, an electric heater is often the practical choice: no flue, no smoke near neighbours and no log store. It needs a supply installed by a qualified electrician, sized for the heater; tell us the model and we tell you what it needs. A wood stove works without mains power and gives the traditional fire, but needs a safe flue route and dry log storage. Both the 1.7 m barrel and the pod come with heat-resistant plates for the heater; on Sauna S the stove and its cables are not part of the set. Ask us which heater we can supply for your model. Full comparison: [Electric or wood-burning outdoor sauna: which to choose](/electric-or-wood-burning-outdoor-sauna/) [CHECK slug].

## What should you prepare before delivery?

1. **Measure and mark out** the footprint, door, terrace and clearances.
2. **Build the base** and check it is level in both directions.
3. **Plan the heater:** book the electrician for an electric heater, or plan the flue route for a stove.
4. **Check local rules** for the sauna, electrics and stove with your local authority and installer.
5. **Plan the delivery route:** a clear path for the parts and space to unload. Transport is calculated per order, and we deliver to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe.
6. **Plan the finish:** treat the outside as you would any exterior timber and keep the vents clear.

For budgeting, read [how much a garden sauna costs](https://ideaswood.eu/how-much-does-a-garden-sauna-cost/). Options are priced separately.

## FAQ

### What is the smallest garden sauna you supply?
The Sauna barrel 1.7 m: 1.97 m wide, 1.7 m deep and 2.08 m high, listed for 2-4 people. The next smallest is the Sauna Pod at 2.4 x 1.9 m.

### How much space does a small garden sauna need?
Our compact models cover about 3.4 to 8 m². Add room to walk round the sauna, open the door, step out and keep the heater clearances.

### What base do I need for a garden sauna?
A firm, level, dry base under the whole sauna. For the pod, paving slabs or a small concrete slab are suitable. Ask us for the base details for your model.

### Does a small sauna need electricity?
Only for an electric heater or lighting. An electric heater needs a supply installed by a qualified electrician. A wood stove needs a flue instead.

### Do I need permission for a garden sauna?
Rules differ by country and area. Check with your local authority before you order, especially about size, height, distance to boundaries, stoves and electrics.
