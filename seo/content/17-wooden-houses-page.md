## Log cabin to live in: what to check first
Many people search for "a log cabin to live in". A building you live in is treated differently from a garden room:
1. **Planning permission.** A building used as a separate home needs it. Rules differ in England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland and Ireland. Start with our [link: planning permission guide] (England) and your local authority.
2. **Building regulations.** A home needs approval, including for insulation, heating and electrics. Electrical work must meet Part P in England and be done by a registered electrician.
3. **Wall and insulation.** Our thickest log walls are 68 mm. Check what insulation the model and set include. Ask us for the insulation specification of the model you like. [CHECK: Astra/Justinas to supply the insulation details per model before any claim is made.]
4. **Base and services.** A house needs a foundation and utility connections that are not part of the kit. [CHECK: confirm what the kit includes.]
5. **Total budget.** The kit, transport, base, insulation, electrics and finishes are separate costs. See [link: how much does a garden cabin cost].
