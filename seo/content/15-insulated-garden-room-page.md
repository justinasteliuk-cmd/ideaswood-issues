## H1: Insulated Garden Rooms and Garden Offices
A garden room you can use through more of the year starts with the right wall, floor and roof. Our timber garden rooms come as kits in spruce and pine, in 44, 58 and 68 mm walls, with insulation available as a separate option so you pay only for what you need.

### Choose by how you will use it
| Use | Wall | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Garden office, studio, gym | 44 mm | Our most common size; add floor and roof insulation and a heater for colder months |
| Guest room, hobby room used often | 58 mm | More mass and better heat holding |
| Living space, holiday let | 68 mm | Thickest logs, best heat holding and sound reduction |

### What insulation does and does not do
Most heat is lost through the floor and roof, so insulating them often matters more than thicker walls. Insulation and other options are priced separately. [CHECK: which insulation options each model offers.]
