### Wooden carport kit
A carport has open sides, so it is cheaper and quicker to build than a garage, and it keeps rain and sun off the car. Several models join a closed garage with a carport or a covered terrace under one roof, which suits a car plus bikes, tools or a seating spot. The range gives 13 to 50 m2 under cover. ### BBQ grill hut (grill cabin)
A grill cabin is a covered dining room built around a fire. Guests sit on benches round the grill, so the cook stays part of the evening, in any weather. Models run from 4.5 to 16.5 m2 with 45 mm pine walls and a bitumen-shingle roof. Seating is from 3-5 up to 15-21 people. Standard 6- and 8-corner grill sets and the Luxury set fit the cabins, and the product pages list firebox, hood and chimney sizes.
