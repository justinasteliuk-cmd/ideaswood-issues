### FAQ
**Do you sell wooden garage kits?** Yes. Every model in the garage and carport range uses 44 mm solid log walls on a pine frame, with footprints from 3 x 5 m to 8.8 x 5.8 m.
**What is the difference between a garage and a carport?** A garage is fully closed and locks; a carport has open sides, is cheaper and quicker to build, and keeps rain and sun off the car. Some models combine both under one roof.
**Do you deliver to the UK?** Yes, as a kit; we also deliver to Ireland and the rest of Europe. Transport is calculated for each order. The UK is outside the EU, so customs and import VAT apply.
**Are options and insulation included?** No. Options and insulation are priced separately from the building.
**Do you sell to resellers?** Yes, at wholesale prices after a signed trade agreement, from one product.
