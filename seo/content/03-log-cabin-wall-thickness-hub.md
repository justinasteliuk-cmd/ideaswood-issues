## H1: Log Cabin Wall Thickness: Which Do You Need?
Wall thickness changes how warm, quiet and strong a cabin is, and how much it costs. Our garden cabins come in 44, 58 and 68 mm. Sheds come in 28 and 44 mm.

| Wall thickness | Good for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 28 mm | Sheds and storage | Light and economical. Not for living or heating. |
| 44 mm | Garden office, studio, gym, summer use | Most of our cabins are 44 mm. Add floor and roof insulation and a heater for use in cold months. |
| 58 mm | Guest room, hobby room used often | More mass, better heat holding than 44 mm. |
| 68 mm | Living space, holiday let | The thickest logs. Best heat holding and sound reduction. |

## What thickness does not decide
Thickness is only one part of how warm a cabin is.
- **Floor and roof.** Most heat is lost through the floor and roof. Insulating them often matters more than adding wall thickness.
- **Windows and doors.** See [link: PVC windows for log cabins] for the glazing we use.
- **Draughts and joints.** Tight log profiles and careful assembly keep wind out.
- **Heating.** A small heater is enough for a well-insulated garden office.

## How to choose
1. **Decide the use.** A shed, a summer retreat, an office, a guest room or a holiday let?
2. **Decide the months.** Only summer, spring to autumn, or all year?
3. **Pick the thickness.** 44 mm for most garden offices; 58 or 68 mm if you want more comfort in cold weather or more mass.
4. **Add insulation.** See [link: insulation kits].
5. **Check the rules.** See [link: planning permission guide] before you choose a size.

## Wall thickness questions
**Is 44 mm thick enough for a garden office?** Yes, with floor and roof insulation and a heater, for most UK conditions. 58 or 68 mm gives extra comfort.
**Is a thicker cabin more expensive?** Generally yes, for the same size. See [link: cost guide] for the price range by thickness.
**Can I live in a log cabin?** Possibly, with the right wall, insulation, heating and permissions. A home to live in needs planning permission and building regulations approval.
