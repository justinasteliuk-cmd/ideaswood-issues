### How to choose
- **Space:** barrels are long and round; measure the length plus room to walk around and for any terrace [CHECK: 0.6 m terrace on a 5.9 m barrel, live-category-facts].
- **People:** pick the length by how many will use it at once; check the listed capacity on the product page.
- **Pod, barrel or cabin:** a pod is the compact, boxy option (2.4 x 1.9 m); a barrel gives more length and the 5.9 m version adds a changing room; cabin saunas suit a larger garden or more people.
- **Wood:** pine and spruce; a thermowood version exists for the 1.7 m barrel [CHECK per model]. Read about thermowood: [link /thermowood-cladding-for-timber-buildings/]. (Reviewer: removed the unsourced "more resistant to moisture and rot" claim.)
- **Heater:** wood stove or electric. A wood stove needs a flue and fuel storage; an electric heater needs a suitable supply (see below).
- **Wall thickness:** 44-45 mm on the outdoor models [CHECK: live-category-facts saw 28, 44 or 68 mm options on the sauna pod page; content/08 says 44-45 mm. Resolve before naming options].

### What to prepare
- **Base:** a level, firm base is needed; a barrel needs a stable, level footing [CHECK recommended base per model, e.g. slab, joists]. The base is not included in the kit price [CHECK].

- **Wood stove:** needs a safe flue route and clearance from walls and trees [CHECK stove and flue supplied/included].

### Safety note
A sauna is a hot, enclosed space. Follow the heater and stove instructions supplied, keep flammable items away from the heater and never leave a lit stove unattended. Our saunas are supplied without CE certification; ask your installer about local requirements for electrics, stoves and distance to boundaries before you order.
