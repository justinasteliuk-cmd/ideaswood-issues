### How to choose
- **Space:** barrels are long and round; measure the length plus room to walk around and for any terrace (the 5.9 m barrel has a 0.6 m terrace).
- **People:** pick the length by how many will use it at once; check the listed capacity on the product page.
- **Pod or barrel:** a pod is the compact, boxy option (2.4 x 1.9 m); a barrel gives more length and the changing-room version adds a separate space.
- **Wood:** spruce, pine or thermowood depending on model [CHECK per model]. Thermowood is described as more resistant to moisture and rot outdoors, spruce as lighter in colour [CHECK wording with supplier].
- **Heater:** wood stove or electric. A wood stove needs a flue and fuel storage; an electric heater needs a suitable supply (see below).
- **Wall thickness (pods):** 28, 44 or 68 mm; thicker walls cost more [CHECK price differences].

### What to prepare
- **Base:** a level, firm base is needed; a barrel needs a stable, level footing [CHECK recommended base per model, e.g. slab, joists]. The base is not included in the kit price [CHECK].
- **Electric heater:** needs a power supply installed by a qualified electrician. Ask us for the heater's requirements; we give no power figures here [CHECK].
- **Wood stove:** needs a safe flue route and clearance from walls and trees [CHECK stove and flue supplied/included].
- **Delivery:** transport is calculated per order. Plan a clear route and unloading space.
- **Assembly:** kit assembly time [CHECK]; a layout can be planned in our IdeasPlanner 3D.

### Safety note
A sauna is a hot, enclosed space. Follow the heater and stove instructions supplied, keep flammable items away from the heater and never leave a lit stove unattended. Our saunas are supplied without CE certification; ask your installer about local requirements for electrics, stoves and distance to boundaries before you order.
