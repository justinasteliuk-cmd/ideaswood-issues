## Buying guide (below the grid, about 330 words)

### Summer house, garden cabin or chalet: what is the difference?
A garden cabin is usually one room for daily use, such as an office or studio. A summer house is larger, often has several rooms and a terrace, and is meant for stays in the warmer months; "chalet" is the name for the larger, more house-like layouts [CHECK: Justinas/Astra to confirm how the live category separates summer houses, chalets and garden cabins, and which models sit in each]. If you want an office or studio you use often, see our [link: garden cabins] and the [link: insulated garden rooms] page. For storage, see [link: sheds and storage cabins].

### Choosing the wall thickness
Our summer houses have solid log walls from 28 to 68 mm, and the thickness is shown on each product page. [CHECK: content/08 (live catalogue 2026-10-18) gives 1 model at 28 mm, 11 at 44 mm, 8 at 58 mm and 11 at 68 mm; confirm before publishing.] As a guide: 44 mm suits spring to autumn use and, with floor and roof insulation and a heater, a garden office or studio. 58 mm gives more mass for a guest or hobby room used often. 68 mm is the thickest and is the choice for living space. Our [link: wall thickness guide] explains each one.

### Insulation and windows
Most heat is lost through the floor and roof, so insulating them often matters more than adding wall thickness. Insulation and other options are priced separately from the kit. [CHECK: which insulation options each summer-house model offers.] See our [link: insulation kits guide].

### Before you order
- **Size and layout:** draw it in the 3D IdeasPlanner first.
- **Permission:** many garden buildings fit under permitted development in England, but height, boundary distance and use all matter. A summer house used as a home or holiday let needs planning permission and building regulations approval. Read our [link: planning permission guide].
- **Total budget:** the kit, transport, base, electrics and insulation are separate costs. See [link: how much does a garden cabin cost].
- **Transport:** calculated individually for each order.
