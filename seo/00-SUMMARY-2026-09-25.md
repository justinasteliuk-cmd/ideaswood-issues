## THE 5 THINGS BLOCKING PAGE 1 (in order)

1. **Entity confusion.** Google sees ideaswood.eu, ideaswood.com (mirror), www.ideaswood.eu (old shop), factory.ideaswood.eu ("logcabinsfactory.com"), business.ideaswood.eu, 2 Facebook pages, 2 Instagrams, 2 phones, 2 addresses, 2 founding years, and a legal entity (IDEAS for PEOPLE, UAB, ~2 employees, wholesale code) that conflicts with "factory / manufacturer" claims. → Pick ONE story and ONE domain; consolidate everything to it.
2. **Broken/duplicate architecture.** 3 log-cabin category trees, 10 categories that look like soft-404s, 23 unredirected legacy URLs.
3. **Titles that can't rank.** Product titles are model names only ("Verno", "Kaia"), categories say "Buy … in UK" while pricing is EUR.
4. **Missing money pages.** No page targets "log cabin manufacturer", "wholesale", "44mm log cabin", "delivery to UK/Ireland", "planning permission", "cost" – competitors (Eurodita, Satus Baltic, Summerhouse24, Palmako, Dunster House) have all of them.
5. **Zero trust signals off-site.** No reviews, no directory listings, no backlinks.
