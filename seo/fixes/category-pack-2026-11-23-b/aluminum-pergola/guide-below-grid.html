How to choose an aluminium pergola kit

Open or closed. Both kits share the same 4 x 6 m footprint, the same 100 x 100 mm columns, 40 x 80 mm beams and 16 mm polycarbonate roof. The listing names an open and a closed version; ask us what the closed version adds before you order, so you compare like with like.

Wall and ground. A wall-mounted pergola is fixed to the house, so ask a builder to check the wall where the frame is fixed and the ground where the columns stand. In the UK, read our planning permission guide before you build next to the house.

Colour. Columns and beams come painted dark grey, brown or white, so you can match window frames, doors or garden furniture. Choose the colour when you order.

Aluminium or timber. Want a freestanding pergola, a gable roof or glazed sides? Look at the wooden pergola kits: gable-roof models at 3 x 3 m and 4 x 4 m, and Deluxe pergolas from 3 x 3 m to 4 x 6 m with aluminium sliding windows on two sides. Among the pergola cabins, Pergola-Luna-ALU and Pergola-Haga-ALU have an aluminium sliding window system with 4 mm tempered glass. See the full pergola range to compare.

Aluminium pergola FAQ

What sizes are the aluminium pergola kits?

Both kits are 4 x 6 m: Wall Mounted 4 x 6 m and Wall Mounted Closed 4 x 6 m. For other sizes, see the wooden pergolas or ask us.

What is the roof made of?

16 mm matt polycarbonate roof panels, carried on 40 x 80 mm aluminium beams and 100 x 100 mm aluminium columns.

Do you have a freestanding aluminium pergola?

The current listing has wall-mounted kits only. For a freestanding pergola, choose one of the wooden pergola models, or contact us with your size.

Can a business buy aluminium pergola kits wholesale?

Yes. As a supplier and exporter we sell to B2B partners such as garden retailers and builders after a signed trade agreement, from one product. See trade partners.
