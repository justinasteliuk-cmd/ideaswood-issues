Our aluminium pergola kits are wall-mounted: the frame is fixed to the house and covers a 4 x 6 m terrace. Two versions are listed, Wall Mounted 4 x 6 m and Wall Mounted Closed 4 x 6 m. Both use 100 x 100 mm aluminium columns with 2.5 mm thick walls, 40 x 80 mm beams and 16 mm matt polycarbonate roof panels, painted dark grey, brown or white. Each aluminium pergola kit can be delivered to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe. Options are priced separately and transport is calculated per order. If you would rather build in timber, the wooden pergolas run from 3 x 3 m to 4 x 6 m.
