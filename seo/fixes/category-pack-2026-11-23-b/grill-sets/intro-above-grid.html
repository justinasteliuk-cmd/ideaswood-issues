These BBQ grill sets are the centre of a grill cabin: a firebox with a table round it, a smoke hood and a smoke pipe. Five models are listed. The Standard BBQ Grill comes in a 6-corner and an 8-corner version, the Luxury BBQ Grill has 6 corners, a Corten steel ash tray and a matt black powder coat, and the round Flame BBQ and Large Flame BBQ are listed at 1.06 m and 1.4 m outside diameter. Cooking grates are stainless steel on the Standard grills. Options are priced separately and transport is calculated per order.
