How to choose a BBQ grill set

Standard 6-corner or 8-corner. Both Standard grills have a cold-rolled steel firebox in heat-resistant black paint (RAL 9005), a grill body and smoke hood in heat-resistant brown (RAL 8019) and stainless steel cooking grates. Grill height from the ground is 604 mm on both.

  • 6-corner: firebox diagonal 780 mm, table diagonal 1380 mm (1660 mm extended), smoke pipe 2 x 900 mm. A large 480 mm grate for meat, fish and vegetables, plus two round 195 mm grates for a coffee or soup pot.
  • 8-corner: firebox diagonal 945 mm, table diagonal 1515 mm (1775 mm extended), smoke pipe 2 x 1100 mm.

Luxury. The Luxury BBQ Grill has 6 corners, a 1 mm steel grill body, a 2 mm firebox, a 5 mm Corten steel ash tray, stainless steel grate stands and a 0.9 mm galvanised steel smoke hood, powder coated in heat-resistant matt black. It comes with a table round the grill, an extendable chimney, two grates and a safety fence for the grill table. Height from the ground is 644 mm, firebox diagonal 800 mm, table diagonal 1380 mm (1660 mm extended).

Flame BBQ. Flame BBQ (1.06 m outside diameter, 1.18 m high) and Large Flame BBQ (1.4 m, 1.2 m high) are the round models.

Fit and safety. Each BBQ grill cabin already includes a central grill in its set; a separate grill set suits a replacement, an upgrade or your own building. Tell us the cabin or room size and we confirm which grill fits. Follow the clearances in the grill manual and local fire rules for the flue.

Grill set FAQ

What is the difference between the Standard and the Luxury grill?

The Luxury grill is listed with a 2 mm firebox, a 5 mm Corten steel ash tray, a galvanised steel hood, a matt black powder coat, two grates and a table safety fence, and stands 644 mm high. The Standard grills are painted in heat-resistant black and brown, have stainless steel cooking grates and stand 604 mm high.

Which grill has the largest table?

The Standard 8-corner: 1515 mm table diagonal, 1775 mm extended.

How big are the Flame BBQ models?

Flame BBQ is 1.06 m across and 1.18 m high; Large Flame BBQ is 1.4 m across and 1.2 m high.
