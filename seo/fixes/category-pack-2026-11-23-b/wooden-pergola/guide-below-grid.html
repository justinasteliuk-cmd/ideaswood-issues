How to choose a wooden pergola

Gable roof or flat roof. Zulu, Rose, Missy and Oli (3 x 3 m) and Uma and Modu (4 x 4 m) have a gable roof with 19 mm roof boards. Ridge heights run from 2.63 m (Zulu, Missy) to 3.66 m (Uma, Modu), and Zulu stands on 195 x 195 mm columns. The Deluxe pergolas have a flat roof, 140 x 140 mm solid columns and 140 x 44 mm rafters, 2.5 m high, in 3 x 3, 3 x 4, 3 x 5, 3 x 6, 4 x 4, 4 x 5 and 4 x 6 m.

Open or with sides. For a wooden pergola with roof and sides, Deluxe has two sides of anthracite (RAL 7016) aluminium sliding windows with 4 mm safety glass and two sides of 19 mm cladding. Glazed and solid sides can swap places during assembly to suit the view. Pergola-Luna-ALU and Pergola-Haga-ALU have an aluminium sliding window system with 4 mm tempered glass.

Pergola cabins. Linsi and Luna (3 x 4 m) and Haga and Kipa (4 x 4 m) are listed with 68 mm walls, 19 mm roof boards and 28 mm floor boards. Compare them in pergola cabins.

Base and planning. Fix the posts to a firm base as the manual shows. Deluxe uses Rothoblaas column supports on a concrete foundation or a wooden base. In the UK, read our planning permission guide before you build next to the house.

Wooden pergola FAQ

What sizes do your wooden pergola kits come in?

From 3 x 3 m to 4 x 6 m: gable-roof models at 3 x 3 and 4 x 4 m, pergola cabins at 3 x 4 and 4 x 4 m, and Deluxe from 3 x 3 m up to 4 x 6 m.

Does every wooden pergola have a roof?

Yes. The gable-roof models, the pergola cabins and Deluxe all have a roof of 19 mm boards. Deluxe has a flat roof; Zulu, Rose, Missy, Oli, Uma and Modu have a gable roof.

Is the timber treated?

Deluxe wood is supplied untreated, so plan to finish it after assembly. For other models, ask us when you order.

Yes. As a supplier and exporter we sell wholesale to B2B partners after a signed trade agreement, from one product. See trade partners.
