Choose a wooden pergola kit for a terrace, a garden dining corner or a quiet seat at the end of the lawn. The range runs from 3 x 3 m to 4 x 6 m in three types: gable-roof timber pergolas (Zulu, Rose, Missy and Oli at 3 x 3 m, Uma and Modu at 4 x 4 m), flat-roof Deluxe pergolas with aluminium sliding windows on two sides, and pergola cabins on 68 mm timber (Linsi, Luna, Haga and Kipa). Every model has a roof of 19 mm boards. Each timber pergola kit is delivered to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe. Options are priced separately and transport is calculated per order.
