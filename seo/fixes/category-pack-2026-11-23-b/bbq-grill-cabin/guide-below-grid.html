How to choose a grill cabin

Size and seats. Count the people you usually host, then pick the internal area:

  • Grill Cabin 4.5 m²: 3–5 people, 3 benches
  • Grill Cabin 6.9 m²: 10–15 people, 5 benches
  • Exclusive grill cabin 7.0 m²: 10–15 people, 5 benches
  • Grill Cabin 9.2 m²: 12–15 people, 5 benches
  • Grill Cabin 14.9 m²: 14–20 people, 5 benches
  • Grill Cabin 16.5 m²: 15–21 people, 7 benches

From 6.9 m² up, two of the benches extend for sleeping. Heights run from 2.57 m (4.5 m²) to 3.55 m (14.9 and 16.5 m²), with 1.2 m side walls on every model.

Standard or Exclusive. The standard cabins have straight walls, floor panels, a standard central BBQ and an extendable standard chimney. The Exclusive grill cabin 7.0 m² has sloping walls, a 28 mm tongue-and-groove floor, a central luxury BBQ and an extendable luxury chimney.

The grill. Separate grill sets are listed too: Standard 6-corner and 8-corner grills, a Luxury grill and the round Flame BBQ models. Tell us your cabin and we confirm which grill fits.

Base and flue. Set the cabin on a firm, level base, follow the clearances in the grill manual and check local fire rules for the chimney. In the UK, read our planning permission guide.

Pair it with a wood-fired hot tub, a pergola or garden furniture.

BBQ hut FAQ

Is a Scandinavian BBQ hut the same as a grill cabin?

It is the same kind of building: a wooden hut with the grill in the centre and benches round it. We call ours grill cabins; sizes run from 4.5 to 16.5 m².

Is the grill included in a grill cabin?

Yes. Each cabin set includes a central BBQ with a table fitted around it, benches with cushions and an extendable chimney.

Do you have BBQ huts for sale in the UK?

Yes. We deliver grill cabins as kits to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe, and transport is calculated per order.

Can holiday parks or retailers buy grill cabins wholesale?

Yes. As a supplier and exporter we sell to B2B partners after a signed trade agreement, from one product. See trade partners.
