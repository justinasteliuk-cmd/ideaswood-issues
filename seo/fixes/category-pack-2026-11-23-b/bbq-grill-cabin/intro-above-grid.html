A BBQ grill cabin is a wooden hut built around a fire: the grill stands in the middle, a table runs round it and the benches face the cook, so everyone eats together whatever the weather. We list six grill cabins from 4.5 m² for 3–5 people to 16.5 m² for 15–21 people. Each wooden BBQ hut has a timber frame with 45 mm pinewood walls, a bitumen-shingle roof, double-glazed windows and a door with a hexagon window. The central grill, table, benches, cushions and extendable chimney are in the set. Options are priced separately and transport is calculated per order.
