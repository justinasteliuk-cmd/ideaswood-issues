A pergola cabin gives a covered garden space with more shelter than an open pergola. This category lists 13 models from 3 x 3 m to 4 x 6 m. Linsi and Luna (3 x 4 m) and Haga and Kipa (4 x 4 m) are listed with 68 mm walls, 19 mm roof boards and 28 mm floor boards. Pergola-Luna-ALU and Pergola-Haga-ALU add an aluminium sliding window system with 4 mm tempered glass. The flat-roof Deluxe pergolas, from 3 x 3 m to 4 x 6 m, have glazed sliding windows on two sides. Options are priced separately and transport is calculated per order.
