How to choose a pergola

Wood or aluminium. The wooden pergolas cover most sizes and shapes: Zulu, Rose, Missy and Oli at 3 x 3 m and Uma and Modu at 4 x 4 m, all with a gable roof of 19 mm boards. The aluminium pergola kits are wall-mounted, 4 x 6 m, with 100 x 100 mm aluminium columns, 16 mm matt polycarbonate roof panels and a choice of dark grey, brown or white paint.

Freestanding or wall-mounted. A freestanding pergola can go anywhere on the plot; choose one of the wooden models. To cover a terrace against the house, the aluminium kits are fixed to the wall, in an open and a closed version.

Open or with sides. Deluxe pergolas (3 x 3 m to 4 x 6 m, flat roof, 2.5 m high) have two sides of aluminium sliding windows with 4 mm safety glass and two sides of 19 mm cladding, and the sides can swap places during assembly. The pergola cabins Linsi and Luna (3 x 4 m) and Haga and Kipa (4 x 4 m) are listed with 68 mm walls; the ALU versions of Luna and Haga add an aluminium sliding window system.

Base and planning. Fix the posts to a firm base as the manual shows. In the UK, read our planning permission guide before you build next to the house. Then furnish it with wooden garden furniture.

Pergola FAQ

What sizes are your pergola kits?

From 3 x 3 m to 4 x 6 m. Wooden gable-roof models are 3 x 3 or 4 x 4 m, pergola cabins 3 x 4 or 4 x 4 m, Deluxe 3 x 3 to 4 x 6 m, and the aluminium pergolas 4 x 6 m.

Should I choose a wooden or an aluminium pergola?

Choose aluminium for a wall-mounted 4 x 6 m cover with a polycarbonate roof. Choose wood for a freestanding pergola, a gable roof, glazed sliding sides or a smaller size.

Do you deliver pergolas to the UK and Ireland?

Yes. We deliver pergola kits to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe, and transport is calculated per order.

Yes. As a supplier and exporter we work with B2B partners such as garden retailers, builders and holiday parks after a signed trade agreement, from one product. See trade partners.
