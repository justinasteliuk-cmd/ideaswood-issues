Pergola kits for a garden, a terrace or the wall of the house, from 3 x 3 m to 4 x 6 m. The range has 21 models in four groups: wooden pergolas with a gable roof, flat-roof Deluxe pergolas with glazed aluminium sliding windows on two sides, pergola cabins on 68 mm timber, and two wall-mounted aluminium pergolas at 4 x 6 m with a polycarbonate roof. Every pergola kit is delivered to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe. Options are priced separately and transport is calculated per order, so you pay for what you choose.
