Planning permission questions

Do I need planning permission for a log cabin holiday let?

Usually yes. Permitted development only covers a garden building used for a purpose incidental to your house, not separate living accommodation [1] [2]. Letting it to guests is a different use, so apply to your local planning authority first [5]. Building regulations also apply if it has sleeping accommodation [6].

Do I need planning permission for holiday log cabins in an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty?

Yes, expect a full application. National policy says development in National Landscapes (the new name for AONBs), National Parks and the Broads should be limited in scale and extent, and gives substantial weight to conserving their natural beauty [7]. Ask the council or National Park authority for pre-application advice.

Do you need planning permission to build two log cabins as holiday lets in Scotland?

Expect to. Planning permission is required for all development [9], and every short-term let needs a short-term let licence from the council before it takes bookings [12]. A building warrant is a separate approval [15] [19]. Talk to your council’s planning and building standards teams.

Do I need planning permission for a log cabin in my garden?

Often not in England, if it meets the height, position, size and use rules above [2]. Within 2 m of a boundary, keep the total height at or below 2.5 m, measured from the ground. In Scotland the limits differ: 4 m overall, 2.5 m within 1 m of a boundary, and 8 m² in a conservation area [16] [18]. Check with your local planning authority if any rule does not fit.

Can I live in a log cabin permanently?

Not under permitted development. A cabin used as a separate home needs planning permission [2] [3], and it is not exempt from building regulations because it contains sleeping accommodation [6]. Ask your local planning authority before you plan to live in one.

Can I use a garden cabin as an office?

Often yes, if it is used by your household alongside the house. Official guidance does not name office use explicitly, so if you are unsure, ask your council or apply for a Lawful Development Certificate.

Can I sleep in my log cabin?

Permitted development does not cover a cabin used as a bedroom or as separate living accommodation, and a building with sleeping accommodation is not exempt from building regulations. Ask your council before you plan any sleeping use.

Do I need permission for a sauna or a garage?

The Planning Portal lists sauna cabins and garages among outbuildings, so the same rules apply. Check the height limit for your roof type and the position rule.

Does the cabin need a foundation?

Yes, every cabin needs a firm, level base. The base is arranged locally and is not part of the kit. Count its height when you check the 2.5 m limit.
