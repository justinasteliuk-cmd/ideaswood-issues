IdeasWood company facts

Last updated: 21 November 2026

IdeasWood is a supplier and exporter of log cabins, garden rooms, saunas and camping pods, based in Lithuania and trading since 2007. IdeasWood sells to private buyers and, at wholesale prices, to B2B resellers.

Who we are

  • IdeasWood was founded in 2007.
  • IdeasWood is a supplier and exporter.
  • IdeasWood is based in Didžiosios Riešės, Vilnius district, Lithuania.

What we supply

  • IdeasWood supplies log cabins.
  • IdeasWood supplies garden rooms and garden offices.
  • IdeasWood supplies saunas.
  • IdeasWood supplies camping pods.
  • IdeasWood products are made from spruce and pine from northern Europe.
  • IdeasWood garden cabins come with walls from 28 mm to 68 mm; most models have 44 mm, 58 mm or 68 mm walls.
  • Every IdeasWood kit is cut to order.
  • Every IdeasWood kit comes with assembly instructions.

Who we serve

  • IdeasWood sells to private buyers who want a cabin, garden room or sauna for their own garden.
  • IdeasWood sells to businesses that resell garden buildings in their own country.
  • IdeasWood exports outside Lithuania, to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe, and further afield.

How ordering works

  1. Choose a model, or plan a layout in 3D with IdeasPlanner.
  2. Choose insulation and other options; each is priced separately.
  3. Send your delivery address; IdeasWood calculates transport for your order.
  4. Your kit is cut to order.
  5. The kit is delivered with assembly instructions.

Delivery

  • IdeasWood calculates transport separately for each order.
  • The transport price depends on what you order and where it goes.

Wholesale and trade partners

  • IdeasWood sells at wholesale prices to B2B partners.
  • Wholesale prices apply after a trade agreement is signed.
  • A trade partner can order from one product.
