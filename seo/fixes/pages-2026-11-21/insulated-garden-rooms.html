What insulation does and does not do

Most heat is lost through the floor and roof, so insulating them often matters more than thicker walls. Insulation kits and other options are priced separately.

Garden room questions

Can I use a garden room as an office?

Yes. Choose 44 mm or thicker, add floor and roof insulation and heating. Check permission rules first.

Do you supply insulation?

Yes, insulation kits are available as a separately priced option.

Is 44 mm thick enough?

Yes, for an office or studio in most UK conditions, as long as you add floor and roof insulation and a heater; 58 or 68 mm gives extra comfort.

Can I live in it?

A home to live in needs planning permission and building regulations approval.
