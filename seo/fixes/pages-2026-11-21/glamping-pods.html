What is in the set

The insulated pod comes with insulated walls, an insulated pinewood floor, the window, the door with handle and lock, and a terrace from 28 mm impregnated decking boards. Furniture and electrical installation are not included. The 3 x 5.9 m pod can stand on blocks, a wooden terrace or a trailer, so it can be moved from one place to another; its terrace is an extra option. Insulation and other options are priced separately. Transport is calculated individually for each order.

Camping pod questions

Are the pods insulated?

The 2.4 x 3.0 m pod is insulated with 100 mm stone wool in the floor and walls. The 3 x 5.9 m pod has 28 mm walls without insulation.

How many people does a pod sleep?

The insulated 2.4 x 3.0 m pod is for 2 people. The 3 x 5.9 m pod has two rooms and 15.03 m² inside.

Is furniture included?

No. Furniture and electrical installation are not included in the pod set.

Do you deliver to the UK and Europe?

Yes. Transport is calculated individually for each order.

Can I buy one pod at wholesale price?

Yes, after a trade agreement is signed there is no minimum order.
