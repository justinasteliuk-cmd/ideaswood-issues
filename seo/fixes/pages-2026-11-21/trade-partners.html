Searching for a log cabin, wooden house, sauna or camping pod manufacturer?

If you are looking for a manufacturer in Lithuania to buy log cabins, wooden houses, saunas or camping pods from, IdeasWood is your single point of contact: we supply and export, and you get wholesale prices after a signed trade agreement, from one product. Ask us for a call or a visit.

How it works

  1. Apply. Register as a partner. We check your company details.
  2. Sign the trade agreement. After it is signed, you get access to the wholesale price list for our range.
  3. Order what you need. You can order even a single product. There is no minimum order quantity.
  4. Agree the transport. We calculate transport individually for each order, so you pay for the delivery you actually need.

Who can apply

Retailers and resellers that operate in a country other than Lithuania: garden building shops, garden centres, online shops, holiday-park suppliers and builders who resell. If you are a private buyer, see our garden cabins for retail prices.

What you get as a log cabin wholesale partner

  • Wholesale prices for partners, shown after the trade agreement is signed.
  • Order from one product up: no minimum quantity.
  • The full range: garden cabins, summer houses and chalets, sheds and storage cabins, saunas, camping pods, BBQ grill cabins, A-frame cabins and garden furniture.
  • Transport quoted for each order.
  • Options such as insulation priced separately, so you can quote your own customers exactly what they choose.

Prices and options

Wholesale prices are not shown on this page. They are shared with partners after the agreement. Insulation and other options are priced separately from the cabin. Transport is calculated for each order.
