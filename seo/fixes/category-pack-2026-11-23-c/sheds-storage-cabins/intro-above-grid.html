Wooden garden sheds and storage cabins for tools, bikes, a mower or firewood, supplied as prefab kits and delivered to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe. The 49 models listed here run from the Lulu-1 2.4 x 1.2 m, with 2 m² inside, to the Lynx 5 x 4 with 17.8 m². 47 sheds have 28 mm solid log walls; the Edith 3 x 3 Premium and Elise 3 x 5.9 Premium have 44 mm. Most list 19 mm floor and roof boards, and seven add a small covered terrace. Options are priced separately and transport is calculated per order.
