How to choose a wooden garage or carport

Garage, carport or both. A timber garage is closed and lockable, with room for a car and storage. A wooden carport kit keeps rain and sun off the car with open sides. The Carport+storage 5.7 x 5.3 and 5.7 x 7.9 models add a closed store to the carport, and the Garage+carport 6.8 x 5.6 joins a 17.2 m² garage with a covered carport of the same size.

Size. The listed garages are 3.2 x 5.2 m (14.6 m² inside), 4 x 6 m (21.2 m²), 5 x 5 m (22 m²), 6 x 6 m (32.6 m²), 7 x 5.5 m (34.8 m²) and 6 x 9 m (49.8 m²). The carports Fugi and Niki list 13 m² and 14 m². Compare the internal size on the product page with your car, and leave room to open the car doors and walk round it. Several sizes have a version marked UK.

Walls and roof. Every model lists 44 mm walls. The garages with a full specification are built from pine logs with a bitumen-shingle roof on 19 mm roof boards; side walls are 2.16 to 2.43 m high, depending on the model.

Economic or Premium. The 3.2 x 5.2, 4 x 6, 5 x 5, 6 x 6 and 7 x 5.5 m garages and the Garage+carport 6.8 x 5.6 m come in both sets. The door size differs between the sets; check each product page.

Base and permission

A garage needs a firm, level base under the whole footprint; the base footprint is listed on each product page. Check height, boundary and use rules before you order: read our UK planning permission guide and ask your local planning authority. Plan a delivery route and space to unload.
