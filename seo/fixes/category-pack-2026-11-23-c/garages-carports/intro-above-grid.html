Wooden garage kits, carports and garage-carport combinations, delivered to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe. Sixteen models are listed, all with 44 mm walls: closed timber garages from 3.2 x 5.2 m (14.6 m² inside) to 6 x 9 m (49.8 m²), three carports (Fugi, Niki and Pablo), two carports with a storage room and a garage with a carport under one roof. Six models come in an Economic or a Premium set, and roof boards are 19 mm where listed. Options are priced separately and transport is calculated per order.
