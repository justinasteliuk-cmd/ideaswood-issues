How to choose a wooden hot tub

Stove outside or inside the tub. With an external wood-fired heater the stove stands outside the tub wall, so the fire stays apart from the bathers and the whole tub is free for seating. The round tub with an outside firewood stove, the double hot tub and the square hot tub are listed with an external heater. With an integral stove the heater sits inside the water; that is the round tub with an integral firewood stove.

Wood-fired or electric. The round integral tub lists two heater types: firewood integral or electric. An electric wooden hot tub needs a power supply installed by a qualified electrician. If you cannot burn wood at home, start with this model and ask us about the heater for your order.

People and size. For two, the oval double hot tub is 120 x 190 cm. For a family or guests, the round tubs take 4–8 or 6–10 people. The square hot tub seats seven in 2.2 x 2.2 m and holds 1,500 litres.

What the set lists. The round and double tubs list a basic set with a firewood stove, insulated spa hood, outside water outlet, water filter with cartridge and circulation pump, foam insulation, two metal straps, a 2 m chimney, stair steps and decorative spruce outside. The square tub lists an acrylic insert insulated with foam, air and water massage, LED lights, pop-up speakers, an outdoor wood-burning stove with a 2 m chimney, wood-composite cladding and steps, a filter and an ozonator. Upgrades are priced separately.

Base, water and stove

Place the tub on a firm, level base that can carry it when full, with water nearby to fill it and a route to drain it. Leave room around the stove, follow the stove manual for clearances and the chimney, and keep firewood dry and away from the fire. Plan a delivery route and space to unload.
