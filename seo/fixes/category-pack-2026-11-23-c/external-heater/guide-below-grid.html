Choosing a hot tub with an external wood-fired heater

Why an external heater. An external wood-fired hot tub heater sits beside the tub instead of inside it. You light and feed the stove from outside, the bathers keep their distance from the fire, and the space inside the tub is all seating. If you would rather have the stove in the water, see the round tub with an integral firewood stove in all wooden hot tubs.

Pick the size by the number of people. For two, the double hot tub is oval, 120 x 190 cm and 1 m high, with 650–750 litres. For a family or guests, the round tub with outside firewood stove comes in two diameters: 2 m with 900 litres or 2.25 m with 1,300 litres, 1.15 m high, listed for 6–10 people. The square hot tub is 2.2 x 2.2 m, 1 m high, with 1,500 litres for seven.

What comes in the set. The round and double tubs list a firewood stove, a 2 m chimney, an insulated spa hood, foam insulation, a water filter with cartridge and circulation pump, an outside water outlet, two metal straps, stair steps and decorative spruce outside. The square tub adds an acrylic insert, air and water massage, LED lights and an ozonator, with wood-composite cladding and steps. Upgrades are priced separately.

Space around the stove

Measure the tub plus the stove beside it, and leave room to stand at the stove door. Follow the stove manual for clearances to walls, fences and plants, and for the chimney. Keep a dry, covered place for firewood. The tub needs a firm, level base that carries it when full, water nearby and a route to drain it.

Lights and pumps

The filter pump, and on the square tub the massage systems, lights and ozonator, need a power supply installed by a qualified electrician.
