Hot tubs with an external wood-fired heater: the stove stands outside the tub, so the fire is kept apart from the bathers and the whole tub is free for seating. Three wooden hot tubs are listed with an external heater. The round hot tub with outside firewood stove takes 6–10 people, with Ø 2 m or 2.25 m and 900 or 1,300 litres of water. The oval double hot tub is 120 x 190 cm for two. The square hot tub is 2.2 x 2.2 m, holds 1,500 litres and seats seven. Each listing includes a wood-burning stove and a 2 m chimney. Options are priced separately and transport is calculated per order.
