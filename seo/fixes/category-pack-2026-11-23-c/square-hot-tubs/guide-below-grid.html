Is a square hot tub right for your garden?

Square or round. A square hot tub sits flush against a deck edge, a wall or a fence line, and its straight sides are easy to build a terrace around. Our square hot tub is 2.2 x 2.2 m for seven people. If you prefer a round wooden tub, the round tub with outside firewood stove is Ø 2 m or 2.25 m for 6–10 people, and the oval double tub is 120 x 190 cm for two: see all wooden hot tubs.

Heating. The square tub is listed with an external wood-fired heater: an outdoor wood-burning stove with a 2 m chimney and canopy. You feed the stove from outside the tub, and the whole inside is seating.

Comfort built in. The basic set lists an acrylic square insert insulated with foam, 25 air massage nozzles, 17 water massage nozzles with 15 LEDs, three large LED lights and nine mini LED bulbs inside, pop-up speakers with Bluetooth, a thermometer and control buttons, an electric relay, a special filter, an ozonator and an outside water outlet for draining.

Outside finish. The cladding and the steps are WPC 3D wood composite, with metal corner decoration.

What to prepare

Measure 2.2 x 2.2 m plus room for the stove, the steps and space to walk round. The tub needs a firm, level base that carries it when full, water nearby to fill it and a place to drain it. The massage systems, lights, speakers and ozonator need a power supply installed by a qualified electrician. Follow the stove manual for clearances and the chimney, keep firewood dry, and plan a delivery route with space to unload.
