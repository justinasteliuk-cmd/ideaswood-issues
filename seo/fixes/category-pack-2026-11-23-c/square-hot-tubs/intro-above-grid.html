Our square hot tub seats seven in a 2.2 x 2.2 m footprint, stands 1 m high and holds 1,500 litres of water. It is heated by an outdoor wood-burning stove with a 2 m chimney, so the fire stays outside the tub. The set lists an acrylic insert insulated with foam, an air massage system with 25 nozzles, a water massage system with 17 nozzles, LED lights, pop-up speakers with Bluetooth, a filter and an ozonator. Cladding and steps are in WPC wood composite with metal corner trims. Options are priced separately and transport to the UK, Ireland and Europe is calculated per order.
