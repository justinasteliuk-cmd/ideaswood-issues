Summer house kits and wooden chalets, delivered to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe. The 31 models listed here are larger than a garden cabin: from the Pod 3 x 5.9 with 15 m² inside to the Iberica T3 7.92 x 11.84 with 73.8 m². Many have two to five rooms and a terrace, 15 list shutters, and the Gustav A and B Premium models add an attic room. Solid log walls come in 28, 44, 58 or 68 mm, and several layouts, such as Almeria, Lukas, Orlando and Scarlett, are offered in more than one wall thickness. Options are priced separately and transport is calculated per order.
