<h2>What changes the price</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Size</strong> — the biggest factor by far.</li>
<li><strong>Seasonal or year-round</strong> — an insulated house you can live in costs several times more than a summer cabin of the same size.</li>
<li><strong>Extras</strong> — floor, windows and doors, roof covering, terrace. On many smaller cabins the floor is an option, so compare like with like. For winter use, budget for floor and roof <a href="https://ideaswood.eu/everything-you-need-to-know-about-insulation-kits-for-sheds-cabins-modular-homes/">insulation kits</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Delivery</strong> — depends on where you are; we quote it for your address.</li>
</ul>
