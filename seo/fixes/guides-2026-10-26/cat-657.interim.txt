<strong>How to choose a garden cabin</strong>



Start with how you will use it. A cabin for summer afternoons needs less than a room you will work in through the winter.



<strong>44 mm walls:</strong> 50 models, €2,626 – €12,517. Home office, studio, hobby room. Add floor and roof insulation and a heater for the cold months.

<strong>58–68 mm walls:</strong> 3 models, €10,535 – €15,296. Larger guest cabins. Thicker logs hold heat for longer.



<strong>Size.</strong> Around 10–14 m² inside takes a desk, shelving and a small sofa. For a bed or a meeting table, look at 20 m² and up. Most models have one room; some have two to four, and 15 come with a covered terrace.



<strong>Economic or Premium.</strong> 21 models are sold as an Economic or a Premium set. Both are listed on the product page, so you can compare before you order.



<strong>Base and assembly.</strong> A cabin needs a firm, level base such as a concrete slab, paving slabs or ground screws. See <a href="https://ideaswood.eu/easy-installation/">how assembly works</a>.

<strong>Warranty.</strong> 51 of the 53 models list a 60-month warranty on the product page.

<strong>Garden cabin questions</strong>



<strong>What wall thickness suits a garden office?</strong> 44 mm, with floor and roof insulation and a heater. The logs alone do not keep a room warm in winter.
