A garden office gives you a quiet room to work, a few steps from the house. Our garden office kits are single and two-room log cabins with 44 mm solid timber walls, from 12.3 m² inside (Sydney 3.8 x 3.8) to 21.3 m² (Emily 4.04 x 7.8, with two rooms and a 6.8 m² terrace). Emberly 4.5 x 3.5 adds a 4.1 m² terrace, while Bravik and Oden 5 x 3.5 give 15.1 m² under a 3.51 m ridge. Sydney and Oden come in a Premium set with double glazing. For an insulated garden office, add floor and roof insulation as an option. Options are priced separately and transport to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe is calculated per order.
