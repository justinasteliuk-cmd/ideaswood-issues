How to choose a garden office kit

Size. Start with what goes inside. Sydney 3.8 x 3.8 (12.3 m²) and Emberly 4.5 x 3.5 (13.5 m²) take a desk, shelving and a small sofa. Bravik and Oden 5 x 3.5 (15.1 m²) give room for a second desk. Emily 4.04 x 7.8 has 21.3 m² in two rooms, so you can split a studio and a storage or meeting space. Check the base footprint on each product page and leave space to walk round the building.

Walls and glazing. Every model here has 44 mm log walls with 19 mm floor and roof boards. Sydney and Oden are listed with double glazing; Sydney has two quadruple doors 300 x 198 cm, and Oden has six windows under a gable roof. Emily comes in a standard and a Premium set: compare both on the product pages.

An insulated garden office

If you will work in it through the winter, plan for insulation and heating. Most heat is lost through the floor and roof, so floor and roof insulation matter more than thicker walls. Insulation is an option, priced separately. Read more on our insulated garden rooms page.

Base, power and permission

Each office needs a firm, level base such as a concrete slab, paving slabs or ground screws. Power for lights, sockets and a heater must be installed by a qualified electrician. In England many garden rooms count as permitted development, but limits depend on height, distance to the boundary and use. Read our planning permission guide. Garden office FAQ

What size garden office do I need?

Our garden offices range from 12.3 m² (Sydney) to 21.3 m² in two rooms (Emily). Around 12 to 15 m² suits one person with a desk, shelving and a small sofa.

Is the garden office insulated?

The walls are 44 mm solid logs. Floor and roof insulation are available as options, priced separately, so you choose what your use needs.

Do I need planning permission for a garden office?

Often not in England, if the building meets the height, position and use rules. Rules differ in Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland and Ireland, so check our planning guide and your local authority.

Do you deliver garden office kits to the UK?

Yes. We deliver to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe, and transport is calculated per order.
