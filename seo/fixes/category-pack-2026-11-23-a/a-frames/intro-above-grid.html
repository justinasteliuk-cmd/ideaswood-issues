Our A-frame house kits suit a holiday cabin, a guest house or a retreat by the water. The steep roof comes down to the ground and forms most of the walls, so the room under the ridge is high and open. Choose from three prefab A-frame cabins: A-frame 32 with 32.7 m² inside (including a 6.7 m² attic), A-frame 39 with 35.9 m², and A-frame Moose with 52 m² over 5 rooms, a balcony and a terrace. A-frame 32 and 39 are timber frame buildings in pine, listed for 2–4 people. Every A-frame is supplied as a kit and delivered to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe. Options and insulation are priced separately and transport is calculated per order.
