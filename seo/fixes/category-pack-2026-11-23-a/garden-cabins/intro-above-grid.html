Our garden log cabins come as kits for a home office, studio, gym, hobby room or guest room. Choose from more than 50 log cabins for sale, from 6 m² inside (Eric 3 x 2.5) to 35.1 m² (Padova B 6 x 6.5), with one to four rooms and a terrace on some models. Most cabins have 44 mm solid log walls; Torino 4.5 x 6 has 58 mm walls, and 68 Ottawa 6 x 4.5 and Det 6 x 6 have 68 mm walls. Many models list double-glazed windows and doors. Every cabin is supplied as a log cabin kit and delivered to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe. Options and insulation are priced separately and transport is calculated per order.
