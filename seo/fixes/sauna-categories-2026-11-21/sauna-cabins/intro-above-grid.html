A garden sauna cabin gives you a wooden outdoor sauna with more room to sit, change and cool down. Choose from Pulse, Zen and Nova for 2–4 people, Amber with 16.3 m² inside, or the Sauna S, M, L and XL series. Pulse, Zen, Nova and Amber have 44 mm pine walls. Sauna S to XL are built from 45 x 185 mm profiled spruce and pine beams with a modern low-slope roof and large tempered glazing, in Economic or Premium sets. Sauna L has two rooms and Sauna XL three, both listed for 4–8 people. Options are priced separately, and transport is calculated per order.
