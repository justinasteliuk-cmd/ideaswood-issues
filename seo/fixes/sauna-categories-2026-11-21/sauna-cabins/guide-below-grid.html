How to choose a garden sauna cabin

Start with the space. Sauna S has a 2.09 x 2.09 m sauna room inside a 2.69 x 2.69 m building, the most compact footprint in the S to XL series. Sauna M keeps the same sauna room in a 2.79 x 5.09 m building. Pulse measures 2.39 x 3.34 m with a 0.64 m terrace, and Nova 3.34 x 2.39 m with a 150 x 198 cm glazed panel. Check the building size on each product page and leave room to walk around the cabin.

Then the number of people. Pulse, Zen, Nova, Sauna S and Sauna M are listed for 2–4 people. Sauna L (4.13 x 2.09 m, two rooms) and Sauna XL (3.58 x 4.63 m inside a 4.28 x 7.63 m building, three rooms) are listed for 4–8, with a separate room for changing or resting.

Wall build. Pulse and Nova are panel-built and Zen uses 44 mm wooden logs, all in pine; Amber also has 44 mm walls. Sauna S to XL use 45 x 185 mm profiled beams in spruce and pine.

Wood-fired or electric heater

A wood-fired stove gives the traditional heat and works without mains power, but needs a flue and the clearances in the stove manual. An electric heater needs a supply installed by a qualified electrician. On Sauna S, M, L and XL the stove and cables are not included in the set; safety fences for electric heaters and for wood-burning stoves, and a 24 litre water tank for wood-burning stoves, are listed as options.

Base and electrics

Place the cabin on a firm, level, dry base, keep the vents clear and follow the heater manual for distances to walls and for the flue. Plan a delivery route and space to unload; transport is calculated per order. Budget help: how much a garden sauna costs.

Sauna cabin FAQ

Which sauna cabin suits a small garden?

Sauna S, with a 2.69 x 2.69 m building, or Pulse at 2.39 x 3.34 m. Both are listed for 2–4 people. For an even smaller footprint, see the sauna pod or the 1.7 m barrel sauna.

Which cabins have a changing room?

Sauna L has two rooms and Sauna XL has three, so one room can be used for changing or cooling down.

Is the stove included?

On Sauna S, M, L and XL the stove and its cables are not included in the set. Accessories such as safety fences and a water tank are priced separately.
