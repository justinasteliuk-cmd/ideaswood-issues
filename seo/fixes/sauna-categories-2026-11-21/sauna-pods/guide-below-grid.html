Is a sauna pod right for your garden?

Footprint. The pod is 1.9 m wide and 2.4 m deep, under 5 m² of ground, with 2.6 m of height. That makes it an easy small garden sauna to place in a corner, next to a patio or on a holiday let plot. A terrace is not included, so plan any decking or paving in front of the door yourself.

People. One room for 2–4 people, with portable foot-rest benches. If you want a changing room or space for 4–8, look at the 5.9 m barrel sauna or the larger garden sauna cabins.

Build. Pine panels with 44 mm walls, a 28 mm pine board floor, a bitumen-shingle roof and two 295 x 580 mm tempered double-glazed windows.

Wood-fired or electric heater

A wood-fired stove gives the traditional sauna heat; it needs a safe flue route and the clearances in the stove manual. An electric heater needs a power supply installed by a qualified electrician. The pod comes with heat-resistant plates for the heater or stove. Ask us which heater we can supply for the pod with your order.

Base and electrics

Set the pod on a firm, level, dry base, such as paving slabs or a small concrete slab. Keep the vents clear and follow the heater manual for distances to walls and for the flue. Plan a clear route for delivery and space to unload; transport is calculated per order. For a budget, read how much a garden sauna costs.
