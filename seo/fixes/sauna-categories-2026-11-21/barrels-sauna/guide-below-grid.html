How to choose a barrel sauna

Length decides the use. The 1.7 m barrel is one sauna room, 1.97 m wide and 1.7 m deep, for 2–4 people. It fits where a cabin will not. The 5.9 m barrel has two rooms and 5.10 m² inside: a 1.5 m changing room with a tempered tinted glass door, the sauna room behind it, and side benches in both. It can also be ordered without the terrace or without the changing room.

Wood. Both barrels have 44 mm walls. The 1.7 m barrel is offered in pine or thermowood (read our thermowood guide); the 5.9 m barrel is pine.

Space around it. Allow for the full length plus room to walk round the barrel and open the door. On the 5.9 m barrel the 0.6 m terrace is part of that length.

Wood-fired or electric heater

The 5.9 m barrel is offered with a choice of wood-fired or electric heater. A wood-fired stove gives the traditional barrel sauna heat and needs a safe flue route and the clearances in the stove manual. An electric heater needs a supply installed by a qualified electrician. Both barrels come with heat-resistant plates for the heater; ask us which heater fits the 1.7 m barrel.

Base and electrics

A barrel needs a firm, level, dry base under its full length. Keep the vents clear, follow the heater manual for distances to walls and for the flue, and treat the outside as you would any exterior timber. Plan a delivery route and space to unload; transport is calculated per order. For a budget, read how much a garden sauna costs.

Barrel sauna FAQ

What sizes of outdoor barrel sauna do you offer?

Two: the Sauna barrel 1.7 m and the Sauna Barrel 5.9 m, both 1.97 m in diameter and 2.08 m high.

How many people fit in a barrel sauna?

The 1.7 m barrel is listed for 2–4 people and the 5.9 m barrel for 4–8.

Does the barrel have a changing room?

The 5.9 m barrel has a 1.5 m changing room in front of the sauna room; it can also be ordered without it. The 1.7 m barrel is a single room.

Do you deliver barrel saunas to the UK?

Yes, to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe. Transport is calculated per order.
