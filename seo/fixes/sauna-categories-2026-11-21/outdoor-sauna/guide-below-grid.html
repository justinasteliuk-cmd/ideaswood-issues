How to choose a garden sauna

Barrel, pod or cabin. A barrel sauna is round, 1.97 m across, in a 1.7 m or 5.9 m length. The sauna pod is the compact, square-sided option at 2.4 x 1.9 m. Garden sauna cabins give more floor space and larger glazing, and Sauna L and XL have two or three rooms for changing or cooling down.

Size and people. The 1.7 m barrel, the pod, Pulse, Zen, Nova, Sauna S and Sauna M are listed for 2–4 people. The 5.9 m barrel, Sauna L and Sauna XL are listed for 4–8. Measure the plot with room to walk around the sauna and to open the door, and check the building size on each product page.

Walls and wood. The barrels, the pod, Pulse, Zen, Nova and Amber have 44 mm pine walls; the 1.7 m barrel is also offered in thermowood. Sauna S to XL use 45 x 185 mm profiled spruce and pine beams.

Wood-fired or electric heater

A wood-fired stove gives the traditional sauna heat. It needs a safe flue route, the clearances given in the stove manual and a dry place for logs. An electric outdoor sauna needs a power supply installed by a qualified electrician. On Sauna S, M, L and XL the stove and its cables are not part of the set; safety fences for electric heaters and for wood-burning stoves, and a 24 litre water tank for wood-burning stoves, are listed as options. Ask us which heater suits the model you like.

Base and electrics

Every model needs a firm, level, dry base. Keep the vents clear and follow the heater manual for distances to walls and for the flue. Plan a clear delivery route and space to unload. Outdoor sauna FAQ

What is the smallest garden sauna you supply?

The Sauna barrel 1.7 m (1.97 m wide, 1.7 m deep, 2.08 m high) and the Sauna Pod 2.4 x 1.9 m. Both are listed for 2–4 people.

Is the stove or heater included?

On Sauna S, M, L and XL the stove and its cables are not included in the set. The barrels and the pod come with heat-resistant plates for the heater. Tell us your model and we will confirm the heater for your order.

Do you deliver to the UK and Ireland?

Yes. We deliver to the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe, and transport is calculated per order.
