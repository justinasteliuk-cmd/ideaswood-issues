**H2: Check with your council before you buy**
Every state, and often every council, has its own rules on outbuildings, studios and secondary dwellings: size, height, setbacks, use and whether a building permit or development approval is needed. If your block is in a bushfire-prone area, ask what that means for a timber building too. We cannot tell you what applies to your block, so please speak to your local council or a private building certifier first. **H2: Frequently asked questions**

**Do you sell log cabins in Australia?**
Yes. We supply log cabin kits, granny flat kits, garden offices, saunas, hot tubs, pergolas and garages to Australian addresses. Freight is calculated for your delivery address on every quote.

**Can I change the size or layout of a cabin?**
Yes. Most models can be resized or rearranged. Draw the change in IdeasPlanner or send us your measurements and we will quote the custom version.

**Is insulation included?**
No, insulation is priced separately, so you only pay for it if you need it. It is shown as its own line on the quote.

**Do I need council approval for a cabin or granny flat?**
It depends on your state, your council, the size of the building and how you will use it. Ask your council or a private certifier before you order. We can send drawings and product details to help with the application. [CHECK]

**Can I assemble the kit myself?**
Each kit comes with assembly instructions. Many owners build smaller cabins themselves; for larger buildings, or anything with plumbing or electrics, use licensed trades.

**Do you offer trade prices?**
Yes, to builders and resellers who sign a trade agreement with us. Wholesale prices then apply from a single product.
