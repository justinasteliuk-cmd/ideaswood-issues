**H2: Roof options**
The roof boards come with the kit. For full rain cover, add the EPDM rubber roof membrane, quoted as a separate option. Without it, the boards give shade and some shelter. Ask us about the right option for a wet-climate site.

**H2: Stain it now or later**
Impregnation in clear, pine or white can be added to the quote so the timber has a first protective coat before you build. Ongoing maintenance with a suitable exterior timber finish keeps the colour even.

**H2: Approval for a pergola**
Small freestanding pergolas are sometimes exempt from approval and sometimes not, depending on the size, height, roof, position on the block and your council. An attached pergola may be assessed differently from a freestanding one. We cannot tell you what applies to your property: check with your council or a private certifier before you order.
