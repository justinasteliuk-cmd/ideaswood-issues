**H2: Fibreglass or acrylic liner?**
Both sit inside the timber tub and hold the water. The acrylic liner has a smooth, glossy finish and comes in white, black or blue; fibreglass is the standard liner on the round tubs. If you searched for an acrylic hot tub, this is what we offer: a timber-clad tub with an acrylic inner shell, not a moulded acrylic spa.

**H2: Wood-fired or electric?**
A wood stove needs no power connection and suits rural blocks, farm stays and anyone who enjoys tending a fire. An electric heater or heat pump (offered on the square hot tub) is easier to set and leave. Electric heaters, pumps, jets and lights must be connected by a licensed electrician.

**H2: Before you order**
Plan a level, solid base that can carry the weight of a full tub, keep the chimney clear of trees, eaves and fences, and check with your council about wood-burning appliances and any pool or spa safety barrier rules that may apply on your property. Your council or a certifier can confirm what is needed.
