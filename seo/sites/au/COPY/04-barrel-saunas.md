**H1:** Barrel saunas for the backyard, deck or farm

**Intro:**
A barrel sauna is a round tube of solid timber staves pulled tight with stainless steel bands, with a door at one end. Ours are supplied as kits in pine or thermo-wood, cut to order and packed with assembly instructions, so a barrel can go on a deck, beside the pool or out on an acreage block.

**H2: Pine or thermo-wood?**
Pine is the standard timber. Thermo-wood is heat-treated timber with a darker colour and is offered as an alternative on the product page. Ask us about the price difference for the model you like.

**H2: Wood-fired or electric?**
Both work in a barrel. A wood-burning stove suits sites without a strong power supply and gives the classic crackle; an electric heater is simpler to run and to control. Whichever you choose, the heater, flue and any electrical connection must be installed according to that heater's own instructions, and by licensed trades where required, and your council or certifier can tell you about flues and clearances on your block.

**H2: Placing a barrel sauna**
Sit the barrel on something firm and level: a concrete pad, compacted pavers or a strong deck,, plus access for the delivery and enough clear space around a wood-burning stove's flue. If you plan to put the sauna close to a boundary, check setbacks with your council first.

Yes, the kit comes with assembly instructions. Use a licensed electrician for an electric heater and follow the stove supplier's instructions for a wood-fired heater.
