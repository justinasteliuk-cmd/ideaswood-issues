**H2: Approval comes first**
Granny flat rules are set by each state and applied by each council. They can cover the maximum floor area, the lot size, setbacks, who may live in the granny flat and whether it can be rented out, and the approval pathway. We do not give approval advice, so please speak to your council or a private building certifier before you order, and bring them the model you have in mind. We can supply drawings and product information for the application. [CHECK: documents available]

**H2: Who builds it?**
The kit is designed to be assembled from the instructions, but a granny flat is a habitable building. Most owners hire a builder or carpenter for assembly and licensed trades for plumbing and electrics, and the certifier will tell you which inspections are required.

**H2: Freight to every state**
We quote freight to NSW, Victoria, Queensland, Western Australia, South Australia, Tasmania, the ACT and the Northern Territory. The figure depends on the size of the kit and your delivery address and is shown as its own line.

**H2: Frequently asked questions**

**How much does a granny flat kit cost?**
The kit price for each model is shown on its product page. Your total also depends on the options you add, freight to your address and the local work (base, trades, approval), which is quoted by your own builder.

**Do granny flats need council approval?**
Plan on asking, because the rules differ between states and councils and we cannot assess your block. Check with your council or a private certifier before you buy anything. [Link: /council-approval-guide/ when published]

**Can I rent out a granny flat?**
That is decided by your state and council rules, not by the kit. Ask your council or certifier.

**Can I change the layout?**
Yes. Move walls, doors and windows in IdeasPlanner, or send a sketch, and we will quote your layout.

**Is a timber granny flat insulated?**
Insulation is an option, priced separately. Ask your certifier what your building needs, then add the matching insulation option to your quote.

**Do you work with granny flat builders?**
Yes. Builders and resellers can buy at wholesale prices from one product once a trade agreement is signed.
