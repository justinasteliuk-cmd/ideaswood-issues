**H2: Base, placement and approval**
Every outdoor sauna needs a firm, level, well-drained base. Leave space for the door to open and for safe clearance around a wood stove's flue. Rules on small outbuildings, wood heaters and boundary setbacks differ between councils, so check with yours, or with a private certifier, before you order.

**How much does an outdoor sauna cost?**

**What is the difference between a sauna pod and a barrel sauna?**
A barrel is a round tube of timber staves held by steel bands and comes in two lengths. Our pod is built from panels and its walls are covered with bitumen shingles. Compare sizes on each product page.

**Can I install an outdoor sauna myself?**
The kits come with assembly instructions. Electrical work must be done by a licensed electrician, and wood stoves installed to their own instructions.
