## Rules for using the map
1. Put the P1 phrase of the row's page in the title, the H1 and the first sentence. Put P2 phrases in H2s and FAQ questions, once each. Never repeat a phrase more than twice per page.
2. Do not target phrases for things we do not sell (glulam/industrial capannoni, turnkey construction, infrared saunas, acrylic jacuzzi brands). Write "chiavi in mano" only to say that we supply kits.
3. Price phrases ("prezzi", "costo", "al mq") are answered by the prices already shown on every product card, plus the sentence "isolamento e opzioni a parte, trasporto calcolato per ordine". Never write a price, a range or a delivery time in body text.
4. Permit phrases are informational: answer with "verifica con il tuo comune" and link the GUIDA post. Never state a legal size limit (it varies by comune and region) [CHECK before any number is written].
