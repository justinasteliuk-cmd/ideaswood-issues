## Intro (49 words)
When a cabin is not enough, a log home kit gives you a full house in solid wood. This range covers about 40 designs, from compact plans like Max (18.37 x 21.65 ft) to large plans such as Granada, Porto and Sevilla. Each kit is cut to order and sent with assembly instructions.
