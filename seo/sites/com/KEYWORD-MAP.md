## Phrases we deliberately do not target
- Price caps ("cabin kits for under $10 000", "tiny house kits under $5 000"): only use if a live model fits the number.
- State names and "near me": local intent we cannot serve with a page per state.
- "cedar" (hot tubs, log homes): the site does not list cedar products [CHECK].
- "energy efficient", "eco", "sustainable" modifiers: banned claims.
- "manufacturer", "factory direct": banned wording on .com (the .eu trade page handles B2B searches).
- UK words: "garden room", "summerhouse" as a single word, "log cabin UK": .eu territory.
